Course Context

This course explores modern IoT systems with a focus on edge intelligence, TinyML, and digital twin–enabled architectures. The course examines how intelligent IoT applications are designed and deployed under real-world constraints such as latency, memory, energy, bandwidth, and privacy, moving beyond cloud-centric solutions. Students develop practical system-level understanding through hands-on projects involving deployable edge intelligence.

Complexity Framework

We evaluate projects based on depth: Hardware (L1) → Intelligent Edge (L2) → Cognitive System (L3).
